I joined Sahyadri School in Grade 11.

I am someone who loves being in nature. I enjoy dancing and playing badminton, and over the years, I have also developed an interest in hosting and anchoring events. During my journey at Sahyadri, I was a part of the Newsletter Committee, Culture Committee, and Tuck-shop Committee. Additionally, I was one of the editors of Ninad (2025–26) and a member of the Assembly Team. I joined the Research Club in Grade 11 as well. In Grade 11, I, along with my batch mates, hosted the farewell for our seniors. As a member of the Culture Committee, I had the opportunity to compere and anchor multiple assemblies. I also had the chance to interview several alumni and artists who visited Sahyadri. These experiences have helped me grow more confident, responsible, and have made my journey at Sahyadri truly memorable.

I was always an introverted kid. Not that it's a bad thing, but I really wanted to step out of my comfort zone. I wanted to experience what it was like to live in a boarding school, learn how to tackle different situations independently, and, most importantly, get to know myself. That's why I came to Sahyadri, and it has not let me down. This place has my heart. Although I have spent only two years here, they have truly been the best. I have learned so much about myself that I never thought I needed to. I formed unique bonds and meaningful relationships with my peers and teachers. Every responsibility I took on here taught me something new, and it gave me the courage and confidence to take on the next one. Sahyadri helped me grow in ways I never imagined—it boosted my confidence, taught me to speak my heart out, and the list goes on. It is the little things that made Sahyadri so special to me. Our mornings began with Geet Gunjan, which always filled me with gratitude. We had classes held on the turf, evenings spent playing frisbee with my batch mates, dorm cookouts, hikes, and countless other memories—every moment added something unforgettable to my journey here. I love the campus. I have always loved being in nature, and Sahyadri made me fall in love with it even more. Every corner of the campus has its own vibrant energy and unique charm. At the end of it all, I realize I didn't just spend two years at Sahyadri—I grew here. This place gave me experiences I'll cherish forever, people I'll always be grateful for, and a version of myself I'm proud to become.
