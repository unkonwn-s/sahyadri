I joined Sahyadri in Grade 5 to discover more about myself and how I would fare in life without my parents to help guide and protect me.

During my time on the Student Council as the head of the IT committee, I created the school website, Sahyadri Connect. Alongside this role, I started the school's Crochet Club and helped host the senior Farewell Ceremonies for both the 2023–24 and 2025–26 academic years.

I was also heavily involved in organizing two major events put on by our batch: the Medieval Fest and the SS Fest. On the creative side, I participated in three school theater productions, including two Hindi plays, 'Vriksh' and 'Navras,' and the English play, 'The Twelve Angry Jurors.'

I also had the opportunity to present a school assembly on the Higgs Boson, where I explained the basic concepts of subatomic particles to my peers. Outside of the classroom and school events, I took part in a school trekking trip to Pandav Patthar, reaching an altitude of 12,500 feet.
