I first visited Sahyadri School when I was only three months old, falling in love with the campus and its energy each time I returned until I could finally join as a student in the 4th grade. Today, it completely feels like home. Sahyadri’s unique, low-pressure environment has given me the freedom to dive into everything from sports to the arts.

Over the years, I have actively helped shape our campus culture by acting in plays (including O' Captain, My Captain and Mahabharat ki Gathaye), organizing student events like Friendship Day and New Year celebrations. My time here is defined by balance—combining a love for football camps and Himalayan treks with a deep commitment to sustainability through farming, campus cleaning, and rural outreach programs.
