I joined Sahyadri School in 9th grade.

I enjoy taking part in school activities and contributing to the community. During my time at Sahyadri, I have hosted several school assemblies and served as the anchor for the Dining Hall Committee, Landcare Committee, and School Bakery Committee. I also started the Public Speaking Club to help students build confidence in communication and create a space for sharing ideas.

These experiences have helped me develop my leadership, teamwork, and public speaking skills. I have had the opportunity to work with different groups of people, each with their own unique perspectives and strengths. Through these experiences, I have learned the value of collaboration, adaptability, and effective communication. I am proud of the contributions I have made to the Sahyadri community, and I look forward to continuing to make a positive impact in the years to come.

I joined Sahyadri School because I had always wanted to experience life in a boarding school. My parents also wanted me to study in a KFI school because they appreciated its approach to education. Before coming to Sahyadri, I studied in a Waldorf school, so I have always been part of unconventional learning environments that encourage curiosity, creativity, and personal growth. I have now been at Sahyadri for four years, and it has been a wonderful experience.

One of the things I appreciate the most is how approachable everyone is. The teachers are easy to talk to, and conversations with them feel open and natural. Dorm life has also been one of my favorite parts of being here. Living with friends has helped me build strong relationships and has made everyday life enjoyable.

I also love being surrounded by nature. The beautiful campus makes morning runs refreshing and peaceful, and it is a great place to spend time outdoors. Another passion of mine is playing the sitar, and Sahyadri has given me the space and encouragement to continue pursuing my interests. Overall, Sahyadri has become a place where I have grown both academically and personally. The friendships, the supportive community, and the connection with nature have made my four years here truly memorable.

I am grateful for the opportunities I have had to explore my passions and develop my skills. Sahyadri has provided me with a nurturing environment that has allowed me to grow and thrive.
