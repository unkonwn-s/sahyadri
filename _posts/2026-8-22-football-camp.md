For the past few years, football camp has been the most anticipated sport event in the monsoon term. This year, there was a considerable increase in the participation by both beginners and experienced players throughout all grades.
The camp was conducted by Sameer sir, who has experience in coaching football enthusiasts. He has an academy of his own.
This camp didn’t just help beginners learn football or inexperienced players develop their skills, but also became a medium to make new friendships. Students across grades came together, played as a team, leaving their prejudices behind.
At the end of the camp, we had a tournament. We formed mixed teams of different grades. This gave us another opportunity to play with people from different batches.
Overall, it was an enjoyable experience.
